Shorts Fanout

A private publishing tool for The Deadshot.

Shorts Fanout is a personal tool used by its owner to upload one short vertical video to several of the owner's own YouTube channels, each with its own title, description and tags.

What it does

  • Takes one short video the owner has chosen.
  • Adds a small branding badge and writes a suitable title, description and hashtags for each channel.
  • Uploads the result, by default as private, to YouTube channels the owner has connected with Google sign-in.

It is not a public service: only the owner's own Google account can sign in, and it only ever acts on channels the owner has personally connected.

YouTube access

The tool asks for two YouTube permissions: uploading videos and viewing basic channel information. It does not read or change anything else on the channel. See the Privacy Policy for details.
