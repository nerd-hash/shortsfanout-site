Privacy Policy

Shorts Fanout — last updated 23 October 2026

Shorts Fanout ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner, The Deadshot, to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This page explains what information the app handles.

Information the app handles

  • Google account sign-in: the email address of the owner's Google account, used only to decide who may sign in.
  • YouTube channel details: the name and ID of each channel the owner connects.
  • Authorization tokens: the tokens Google issues when the owner connects a channel, stored in the owner's own private database so the app can upload on the owner's behalf. They are never shown in the app's interface.
  • Videos and their text: the video files the owner chooses, which are kept on the owner's own computer, and the titles, descriptions and tags written for them.

How the information is used

The app uses Google's YouTube permissions only to (1) upload videos to channels the owner has connected and (2) read the name of those channels. It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or any other data, and it does not change or delete existing videos.

Third-party processing

To write titles and captions, the app may send the content of a video the owner chose (its audio, a reduced-quality copy of the video, or a transcript) to Google's Gemini API and to Sarvam AI's speech-to-text API. This content is the owner's own and is used only to produce that text.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and uses it for no purpose other than the uploads described above.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention and deletion

Authorization tokens and channel details are kept in the owner's private database for as long as a channel stays connected. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens. The owner can also remove a connected channel in the app, and may ask for any stored data to be deleted by contacting the address below.

Security

Sign-in is limited to the owner's approved Google account, and stored tokens can only be read by the app's server, never by the browser.
